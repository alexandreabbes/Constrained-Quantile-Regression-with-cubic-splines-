Some inconsistensies concerning polynomial in R

polyder of  library pracma and as.polynomial of library polynom are inconsistent.

For most functions,
as polyder, mkpp, ppeval,
the R convention to handle c(a3,a2,a1,a0) as a  polynomial
is a0+a1x+a2x^2+a2x^3

polyder(c(1,2,3))
[1] 2 2


The convention of decreasing order representation is in contradiction with library polynomial.
as.polynomial(c(1,2,3)) answers
1 + 2*x + 3*x^2

And moreover:
polyder(as.polynomial(c(1,2,3)))
[1] 2 2
as.polynomial(polyder(as.polynomial(c(1,2,3))))
2 + 2*x
instead of 2+6*x
