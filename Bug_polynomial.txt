I have no time to study all the bugs in R concerning the splines and polynomial

Here are some functons that give inconsistent results. For most functions,
as polyder, mkpp, ppeval,
the R convention to handle c(a3,a2,a1,a0) as a  polynomial
is a0+a1x+a2x^2+a2x^3

So far, the polyder function is bugged, and the derivative or order greater that 1 has a big bug. 

polyder(c(1,2,3))
[1] 2 2

the function  as.polynomial(c(3,2,1)) answers
1 + 2*x + 3*x^2

And:
polyder(as.polynomial(c(1,2,3)))
[1] 2 2

so that we also have

as.polynomial(polyder(as.polynomial(c(1,2,3))))
2 + 2*x
